About

I am a Ph.D. student in the Department of Computer Science at Virginia Tech, advised by Prof. Chris North. Before that, I received an M.S. in Software Engineering from Xi'an Jiaotong University and a B.S. in Software Engineering from Northwest A&F University.

My current research is on LLM agents and human–AI interactive systems: how agents reconcile conflicting knowledge in memory, how large language models can ground human intent expressed through spatial interaction, and how to benchmark retrieval-augmented LLMs. Previously, my research focused on deep learning for hyperspectral imaging.

Research interests: LLM agents · agent memory & knowledge conflicts · semantic interaction & visual analytics · LLM evaluation & benchmarking · hyperspectral image denoising (earlier)

About

I am a Ph.D. student in the Department of Computer Science at Virginia Tech, advised by Prof. Chris North. Before that, I received an M.S. in Software Engineering from Xi'an Jiaotong University and a B.S. in Software Engineering from Northwest A&F University.

My research is on LLM agents and human–AI interactive systems: how agents reconcile conflicting knowledge, and how large language models can ground human intent expressed through interaction. I am the author of SoG, a training-free sensemaking layer that resolves conflicting facts in agent memory, and CriterionSI, an LLM-powered system that infers users' clustering criteria from drag interactions. I also work on LLM evaluation and benchmarking. Earlier, I studied deep learning for hyperspectral imaging.
